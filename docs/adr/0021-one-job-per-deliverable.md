# One Job is one deliverable, one signature

Status: accepted

A Job covers exactly one signed deliverable: a Session and its Coordinated Positions Document for the first workflow, a consultation and its report for the second. The document's items — Agenda Items, expert-meeting items — are sections of that one deliverable. The Signer signs once, and one delivery is recorded.

One Job never aggregates other Jobs. There is no annual or multi-year roll-up (0018).

Per-item Jobs would produce forty signatures for a document tabled as a whole, fragmenting the Signer's accountability and making the version history unreadable.

## Consequences

- Job status is deliverable status. There is no per-item approval state in v0.1.
- A forgotten item is a completeness failure of the document, caught by the structural and completeness checks, not by an unapproved Job.
