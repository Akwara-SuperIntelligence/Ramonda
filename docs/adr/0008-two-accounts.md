# Two accounts: Author and Signer

Status: accepted

The desk's team is two people. The Author drafts the Coordinated Positions Document and is answerable for its content. The Signer — the Head of the AU Nairobi office — is the only principal who can release it. There is no broader role hierarchy, no member-state access surface, and no delegated approval in v0.1.

The customer's office, not a 55-state network, is the user. Roles nobody occupies add permission surface with no evidence behind them.

## Consequences

- Every workflow, review surface and state transition is designed for two people looking at the same job.
- Multi-party review is a later client's shape; the clearance model must express it without redesign.
