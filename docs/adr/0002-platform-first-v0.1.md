# v0.1 is the platform; the customer picks the session

Status: accepted

v0.1 builds the platform, not one job taken end to end; the Coordinated Positions Document is what proves it. There is no fixed session and no fixed deadline — past or future is the customer's choice. The system must model the Procedure and the required output well enough to produce a Coordinated Positions Document for whatever session she selects.

Making the session the variable, rather than the exercise, is the stronger test: if a past session can be reproduced and a future one can be drafted, the abstraction is real.

## Consequences

- Session-specific knowledge cannot be hardcoded. The Procedure is a first-class versioned model, and every document derives its structure from Procedure plus agenda rather than from a template.
- The acceptance test is reproduction of a session of the customer's choosing, against her own finished document — figures exact, structure reproduced, prose substantive. Two such documents exist and are the golden case.
