# First client is the AU Codex coordination desk; first job is the Coordinated Positions Document

Status: accepted

The first client is the African Union's Codex coordination desk in Nairobi. The first job is producing the Coordinated Positions document for a Codex session: pull the agenda, committee reports, circular letters, project documents and standard texts from codex.fao.org, corroborate every claim and figure, and emit a signed, audit-ready Coordinated Positions Document for the Head of the AU Nairobi office to defend in Geneva. The second workflow on the same desk — the Continental Expert Consultation Report — is the first proof that a second capability is a module, not a rewrite.

The work is regulated, rule-dense, high-volume and externally deadlined, and its object graph is the AU's own ontology. The agenda, session report, project document and standard text are genuinely independent records of the same decision.

## Consequences

- The typed object graph is about proceedings, not money.
- "Every figure traces to a page" is the defining audit claim, and most figures are statistics rather than money.
