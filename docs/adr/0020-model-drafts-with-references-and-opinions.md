# The model drafts from the internet, with references, and may hold its own opinion

Status: accepted

For each Agenda Item the model drafts Background, African Position, Rationale, Proposed statement and Recommendations. Its sources are pages on the open internet; every factual claim it makes carries a reference to an archived snapshot (0014). Where the sources do not settle a point, the model may state its own opinion. The Author edits the result, and the Signer approves it.

The first client's product is judgement plus evidence, and an opinion that is clearly an opinion is useful to the Author, not dangerous.

## Consequences

- An opinion is not evidence. The document must separate what a source says from what the system judges, and the separation must survive into the signed artifact.
- Every machine-drafted section is marked as such in the version record (0013).
- The Author's edit is the ordinary editorial act; it does not need to remove machine drafting, but the record keeps it.
