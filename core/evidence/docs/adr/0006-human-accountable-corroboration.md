# Corroboration is a human decision, and the system records who made it

Status: accepted

The system does not decide whether a figure may ship. For each figure it shows the provenance, the source candidates it found, and what it rejected. A named person approves the figure or passes the job to the next stage, and that act is recorded against the figure and the job. Where independent sources agree, the system shows it; where only one source exists, it says so. The control is not a policy engine but accountability: every figure has a person who let it through, identifiable years later.

## Considered Options

- **Automatic blocking on any single-source figure (rejected):** stops the desk on statistics that legitimately have one source.
- **Source Authority Register (deferred):** mapping each statistic class to authoritative producers is the right mechanism for larger regulated clients; it is not built now.

## Consequences

- Provenance and the candidate set are first-class data, not a citation string.
- The review surface must show what was found and rejected, not only what was chosen.
- "No second source" is a visible fact about a figure, not an absence of evidence.
